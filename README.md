# Kayole Digital Hub

**Bridging the Digital Divide Through Sustainable Technology Training**

A community-based Computer Literacy Centre and social enterprise located in Kayole Soweto, Nairobi, Kenya.


---

## About the Project

The **Kayole Digital Hub** is a proposed community-based Computer Literacy Centre designed to bridge the growing gap between the rapid expansion of digital technology and the limited ability of low-income communities to access, understand, and use that technology.

The Hub will provide:

- **Affordable computer access** for learners and small businesses
- **Structured digital skills training** through a dual-track model
- **Advanced IT education** in networking, cybersecurity, and AI
- **Practical exposure to artificial intelligence** and emerging technologies
- **Entrepreneurship support** to strengthen local businesses

By combining free community training with paid professional programmes, the Hub will deliver social impact while achieving long-term financial independence.

## Technical Infrastructure

- **20 student workstations** (Core i5, 8 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD)
- **4 staff workstations** (Core i7, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD)
- **24-port managed Gigabit switch** with Cat6 cabling
- **Server/NAS** with automated backup storage
- **Wireless access point** for staff and mobile devices
- **Power protection** (UPS) and **security systems** (CCTV, locks, access control)

### Network Design

The Hub operates on a single IPv4 subnet (192.168.10.0/24) using a star topology. Infrastructure devices use static IPs, while student workstations receive DHCP addresses. The design was verified in Cisco Packet Tracer with a 0% packet loss result.
