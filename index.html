About the Hub

The Kayole Digital Hub is designed to close the growing gap between digital technology and the ability of low-income communities to access, understand, and use it.

Affordable Access

20 student workstations and 4 staff stations, available to the community at low cost.

Structured Training

A dual-track model combining free foundation courses with paid professional programmes.

Future-Ready Skills

Hands-on exposure to AI, cybersecurity, networking, and digital entrepreneurship.

Sustainable Model

A social enterprise designed to achieve full self-sufficiency by Year 2.

Two Tracks, One Mission

Every learner enters through one of two programmes designed to meet them where they are.

Community Track

Free · 4 weeks · 60 learners / year

Foundational digital literacy for youth, women, and beginners. Builds confidence in using computers, the internet, and everyday digital tools.

Pro Track

Paid · 6 months · 40 learners / year

Advanced IT, cybersecurity, and applied AI for careers and business. Includes a capstone project and employability support.
